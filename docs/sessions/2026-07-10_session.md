## What was built (commit `1f7c86e`, pushed)

1. **`src/fea/elements/shell_mitc4.py` (new):** `ShellMITC4` — 4-node FLAT
   shell, 6 DOF/node (ux, uy, uz, rx, ry, rz):
   - **Membrane** = Q4 plane stress (full 2×2 Gauss, reuses `isoparam.py`
     kernels), **bending** = Mindlin-Reissner with `D_b = D_m·t³/12`,
     **transverse shear** = MITC4 assumed covariant interpolation
     (Bathe-Dvorkin tying at edge midpoints A/C for γ_ξz, D/B for γ_ηz,
     each with its OWN Jacobian, pushed to Cartesian with inv(J)ᵀ at the
     integration point), shear correction 5/6.
   - **Plate sign map:** a fiber at +z moves u = z·ry, v = −z·rx, so
     φx = ry, φy = −rx; κ = [φx,x, φy,y, φx,y+φy,x], M = D_b·κ
     (M11 + = tension on the +z face).
   - **Drilling DOF** penalized against the membrane rotation
     ω = (v,x − u,y)/2: `k_drill = drill_alpha·G·t·∫(θz_h − ω_h)² dA`
     (default `drill_alpha=1e-3`). A CONSISTENT rigid rotation about the
     normal has zero penalty energy → exactly 6 RBMs; a θz field alone is
     stiffened at every node (no spurious drilling zeros in flat
     assemblies).
   - **Local frame:** z = normalized cross of the diagonals (node order
     defines the normal — reversing the order flips it and the pressure
     direction, tested), x = edge 1-2 projected, y = z×x. Nodes projected
     onto the mean plane through the centroid; out-of-plane offsets =
     warp, **warns** above `warp_tol` (default 0.05 of mean diagonal);
     analysis always uses the flat projection (v1 scope per plan).
   - **Loads** (`_q0` pattern, local frame): `ShellPressureLoad` (per area
     along LOCAL +z) and `ShellBodyLoad` (GLOBAL per volume × t, e.g.
     self-weight bz=−γ). Bilinear shapes → no fixed-end moments.
   - **Output:** `forces_gp` → (4, 8) `[F11,F22,F12,M11,M22,M12,V13,V23]`
     per unit width at the 2×2 Gauss points, **LOCAL axes** (SAP2000 set;
     local x along edge 1-2 — comparisons must rotate the expected tensor
     into the element frame, see `_to_local_tensor` in the tests).
   - v1 is linear elastic: requires plane_stress `ElasticIsotropic`
     (bending D and shear G derive from its E, ν); layered/composite =
     Phase 5. `k_local` precomputed at `set_domain`.

2. **`src/fea/loads/pattern.py`:** + `ShellPressureLoad`, `ShellBodyLoad`.

## Gotchas found

- `forces_gp` is in ELEMENT LOCAL axes (like SAP2000): on a distorted
  mesh each element's local x follows its own edge 1-2, so a global
  constant stress state reads back rotated per element — rotate the
  expected tensor with Q = R[:2,:2], S_local = Q·S·Qᵀ. (First test draft
  compared globals and "failed" with an exact γ sign flip on 90°-rotated
  elements.)
- The mean-plane projection halves a lifted-corner offset: one corner
  raised h on a flat quad gives warp ≈ (h/2)/diag, not h/diag — size
  warp-warning tests accordingly.
- Hard SS (fix the edge-normal rotation: θx on x=const edges, θy on
  y=const edges) is what converges to Kirchhoff 0.00406; the SAP
  comparison uses soft SS (translations only) to match the .sdb model.
